About me

Building for
the web.

I started out as an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate — substations, power systems, embedded protocols, EV powertrains, the works — before falling headfirst into full-stack web development. Somewhere in between, I picked up vibecoding: describing features to an AI instead of typing every line by hand. It's a genuinely useful way to build things faster, not a replacement for actually knowing how they work.

My web stack spans React.js, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB, backed by AWS Cloud, Docker, and DevOps practices. On the electrical side, I've worked hands-on with power electronics, MATLAB/Simulink, microcontroller architectures, and communication protocols like UART, SPI, and CAN.

I vibecoded a full jewellery management app for our family shop and shipped BrowserKit — a toolkit where everything runs entirely in your browser, so nothing ever gets uploaded and your files stay yours. Both were built quickly by leaning hard on AI tooling, and both still needed real engineering judgment to get right.

These days I split my time between learning LLMs, prompt engineering, agent workflows, and vibecoding new projects and going deeper on the fundamentals — because knowing how to ask well only works if you know enough to tell a good answer from a confident-sounding wrong one.
